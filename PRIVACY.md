<h1>Asgard Gallery privacy policy</h1>
<p>Effective November 26, 2026</p>

<p>Trinadh Thatakula develops Asgard Gallery, a collection of interactive UI components and Kotlin examples for Android and the web. This policy covers the Android app <code>com.valhalla.asgard.app</code> and the public web gallery.</p>

<h2>Using the Android app</h2>
<p>The Android gallery works offline and does not request internet access. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. You can use every example without an account. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Its examples and fonts ship with the app.</p>

<h2>What stays on your device</h2>
<p>The app saves the identifier of the last component you opened in private app storage so it can reopen that preview. Searches, sample form entries and appearance choices are processed locally. Android may retain screen state when you switch apps or rotate your device.</p>
<p>Choosing <strong>Copy</strong> writes the displayed Kotlin example to your system clipboard. The gallery does not read your clipboard. You can clear copied text using your device’s clipboard controls.</p>
<p>To remove saved gallery data, open Android Settings, select Asgard Gallery under Apps, then Storage and Clear storage. Uninstalling also removes the app’s private data. The developer has no server-side profile or gallery history to delete.</p>

<h2>Visiting the web gallery</h2>
<p>Opening the web gallery or this policy sends a request to GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Your browser may cache downloaded files and keep the component link in its browsing history. You can remove these through your browser’s history and site-data settings. The gallery uses no analytics or advertising trackers. <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub Privacy Statement</a></p>
